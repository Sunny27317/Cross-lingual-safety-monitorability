# Final day before upload: QC sequence

Do these in order. Tick each one, and do not skip ahead.

1. **Fill results.** Follow `research/WORKSHOP_V1_RAPID_FILL.md` §Results. Every number
   comes from the frozen analysis output (commit recorded). No hand-typed values.
2. **Select Discussion branches.** Use the triggers in `WORKSHOP_V1_RAPID_FILL.md` and
   `WORKSHOP_V1_DISCUSSION_DECISION_TREE.md`. Delete inactive paragraphs. Note the
   activated IDs in a comment for the record.
3. **Claim ledger.** Every sentence in the Abstract, Results, Discussion and Conclusion
   maps to a row in `WORKSHOP_V1_CLAIM_LEDGER_FINAL.md` whose minimum result holds.
   Exploratory items are labelled.
4. **Citation check.**
   - Every in-text citation has a reference entry, and every reference entry is cited.
   - All entries are VERIFIED (`literature/CITATION_VERIFICATION.md` §A/D/G/H).
   - arXiv versions and venues are re-checked today.
   - Run a quick search for closer work published since 2026-10-04.
5. **Author info.** Name, affiliation, contributions, funding, competing interests and
   acknowledgments, per `WORKSHOP_V1_AUTHORSHIP_DECISION_SHEET.md`, with written
   confirmations and consents on file.
6. **Ethics statement.** ORPI's determination is quoted verbatim, with its date. Rater
   consent and compensation are stated. The two completed text reviews are described
   accurately (the training review was verbal only).
7. **Reproducibility statement.**
   - All stage hashes are filled: generation, direct judge, translation, translated
     judge, human, analysis.
   - The analysis-freeze sentence is true and in the past tense.
   - The executed-generation-code wording is honest (uncommitted at run time; archived
     after).
   - Repository and DOI links resolve.
8. **Figures and tables.**
   - Generated by the frozen code.
   - Intervals shown, models not pooled, denominators stated.
   - Missing and undefined values marked.
   - Urdu renders right-to-left.
9. **Spelling and grammar pass.** Consistent terms: rationale/trace, monitor/judge,
   native-reader reference, G, AG, R, R_full, Cue A/Cue B.
10. **PDF compile.**
    - Clean build; no `??`, no broken references.
    - Urdu font embedded.
    - Appendices A–K attached.
    - Internal comments and `research/…` paths removed.
11. **Final overclaim scan.** Search for: `[[`, the abstract slots (`[PRIMARY_`, `[MAIN_INTERVAL`, `[HUMAN_JUDGE_`, `[DIRECT_VS_`, `[IMPLICATION`), "significant", "p <", "confirm", "prove",
    "first", "novel", "hide", "conceal", "lie", "deceptive", "mitigat", "recover",
    "language-specific", "equivalent", "no difference", "robust across", "ground truth",
    "reliable", "substantial", "moderate", "proves", "cause", "safety failure", "all low-resource", "general multilingual".
    Each hit is removed or justified against the ledger.

**Then:** the investigator reads the full PDF once, end to end, and uploads.
