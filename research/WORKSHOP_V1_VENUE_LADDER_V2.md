# Venue ladder V2 (2026-10-06; no acceptance probabilities)

Assumes the design as frozen: one language, two small models, one judge, one translator, no
paraphrase control, and human validation pending.

| Venue class | Current fit | Minimum additions before submitting | Biggest objection | Realistic strength if human validation is good | What a stronger later version needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Preprint (arXiv)** | Good once results exist | H collected (or the explicit validation-pending variant with no G claims); release-guard gates; privacy and licence remediation | Scope | Citable record of a careful case study | — |
| **Non-archival workshop** (safety, multilingual, or evaluation workshops) | **Best current fit** | Results, including G; the 4–6-page version; Limitations | Generalization | Good; the design and transparency are the selling point | — |
| **Archival workshop** | Good | As above, plus a polished Methods and Appendix J | Small n; one judge | Moderate–good | — |
| **Findings-style track** | Possible | A strong G result with clean κ; the translation audit (S4) done; tight framing | One language, two small models, one judge | Moderate. Depends on how informative G is and on reviewer taste | A second language or second judge would substantially help |
| **Main conference** | **Weak now** | — | Breadth: languages, judges, models | Low with the current design | ≥3 languages including different scripts; ≥2 judges; an English human anchor; a paraphrase control; larger models |
| **TMLR-style journal** | Possible for an expanded version | The expanded design above, plus full reproducibility | Breadth | — | As for main conference; TMLR values correctness over novelty, so a careful expanded version fits |

**Recommended path:**
1. Preprint and non-archival workshop.
2. Incorporate feedback.
3. Expanded study for Findings or TMLR.

Do not submit the same content to two archival venues.
