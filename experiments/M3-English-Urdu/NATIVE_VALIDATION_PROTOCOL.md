# Native Urdu validation — prospective human work required

Native-human validation is central to separating monitor-language limitations from
reasoning disclosure differences. The package supplies forms, blinding, lineage and
comparison code. It supplies no real human labels, verified translation equivalence,
recruitment decision or claim of native competence.

Adopt the [M2 human-reference process](../M2-Monitor-Validation/HUMAN_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.md)
with explicitly approved Urdu qualifications, rubric wording/examples, independent rater
allocation, overlap, adjudication and institutional safeguards. Native/bilingual reviewers
must assess semantic and pragmatic use of the cue rather than relying on machine-translated
labels or Latin answer correctness. Record code-switching, dialect/register and uncertainty
under a prospectively approved policy. Do not define “native” solely by citizenship.

Lock raw independent annotations and reference labels before automated judge outputs are
shown to reviewers. Use opaque IDs and random order; keep model/condition and effect
metadata hidden where compatible with the approved construct. Provide full preserved
reasoning. A reviewer may infer context from text; record this limitation.

For equivalence review, source and translation necessarily reveal language/direction.
Blind translator/provider/version, judge decisions and scientific hypotheses framed as
expected findings. Register whether paired or independent assessment is used and how
it addresses anchoring. Native disclosure annotation and bilingual equivalence audit
serve different purposes and have separate versioned rubrics and provenance.

Predeclare calibration/heldout source-item splits, missingness/abstention treatment,
reference uncertainty propagation and how failures affect scientific interpretation.
No arbitrary agreement cutoff is imposed. Publish pre-adjudication disagreement separately
from adjudicated reference agreement. A human reference is fallible evidence, not proof
of unobservable internal cognition.

**HUMAN REQUIRED before recruitment/collection:** competence criteria, staffing, consent,
compensation, harmful-content process, storage/release policy, institutional determination,
conflict-of-interest and adjudication responsibilities, and explicit stage authorization.
