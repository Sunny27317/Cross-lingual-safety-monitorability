A power calculator does not select a scientifically meaningful effect size. A scenario
that reaches target power under optimistic nuisance assumptions does not validate those
assumptions. Record rejected scenarios and unresolved uncertainty. Do not fill this
record with synthetic-test constants or invent a favourable power justification.
